# 多格建筑形态 · 通用素材完整提示词

使用内置 `image_gen.imagegen`，未使用独立 API。只输入通用素材与造型要求，没有输入账户、日期、贡献数量或真实地块。实际地图由本机程序排布。

第一轮参考 `fantasy-sprites-v1.png`；生成轮廓后发现门窗相对既有小屋过大，再编辑第一轮图集，并以原小屋图集作为人尺度参考。最终选用编辑结果保存为 `fantasy-building-forms-v1.png`，实际为 1254×1254 RGBA。参考小屋风格的来源见 [既有提示词](REAL-RENDER-PROMPT.md)。

生成结果未严格满足等分格内留白和首行方向要求，因此使用 JSON 中逐项裁切与落脚点，不用均分格推算；长条素材按实际方向标定，绘制时水平反射交换两条地面轴。不拉伸像素补长度。原始候选由图像工具管理，仓库只保存最终选用素材。

Each form should occupy its own cell well without touching neighbours. Keep fine crisp details similar to reference. All shadows small and contained. SAME AXIS PROJECTION across all cells: world +X goes screen DOWN-RIGHT at 2 horizontal pixels to 1 vertical pixel, world +Y goes screen DOWN-LEFT at the same slope. Entrances open toward +Y / screen lower-left; never rotate the camera. Single-storey eaves across all forms. Roofs may overlap joined wings naturally, no floating parts.

## 人尺度修订：最终素材

```text
EDIT the FIRST attached 3x3 multi-cell building atlas, using the SECOND attached cottage atlas only as a style and human-scale reference. Preserve its exactly NINE forms and their 3x3 row-major arrangement, transparent background, timber and stone pixel-art style, and broad outer ground contact widths. Fix one critical issue: these multi-cell buildings currently have giant human doorways and tall eaves. They must spread across MORE GROUND at the SAME human scale as little cottages. Keep each sprite's ground footprint wide/long but reduce all wall/roof vertical height above its ground plane by roughly HALF; this is an architectural redraw, NOT simply shrinking whole sprites. Tiny exterior human doors should be only about 8–10 percent of a cell height (35–42 pixels if cells are about 418px high), with normal-sized repeated windows, modest low eaves and narrow steep gable sections. Add more actual structural bays/rooms along long roofs and multiple coherent low wings in manors, rather than making gigantic doors or giant windows. Farm house, barn and stable must each have modest doors and low wall heights. Barn doors may be a little wider, NOT twice human height. Overall silhouettes become lower, with more transparent air above them, while maintaining broad horizontal ground contact span. Same sun direction and clean alpha. Roofs stay three-dimensional and not squashed; use narrower multiple roof runs, repeated bays and low low side wings to achieve this.

Preserve exact contents: R1C1 rustic timber/thatch longhouse; R1C2 refined slate workshop longhall; R1C3 connected plum-roof L-plan winged manor. R2C1 a longer/deeper rustic longhouse; R2C2 a longer/deeper refined workshop longhall; R2C3 blue-roof U-plan guild manor with small central court. R3C1 complete farmhouse + distinct barn + roofed stable + hay/trough compound; R3C2 narrow linear farmhouse + barn/storage wing + lean-to; R3C3 broad connected manor with two low side wings and open front court. NO missing parts, no detached ornamental cottages as substitute for joined wings. No tower, extra storey, letters, borders or base tiles. No ground scenery. All sprites fully isolated in their own equal cell, padding at least 5%; no object or shadow touches adjacent cell. Consistent 2:1 isometric plane; longer first-row halls extend in the down-left direction, and renderer will reflect horizontally where needed. Nine coherent properly scaled low architectural forms on genuinely transparent square background.
```
